# DSM-5 Diagnostic Criteria: Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders

## 1. Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD)
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: F42.2
- **DSM-5 Code Category**: Obsessive-Compulsive and Related Disorders

### Diagnostic Criteria
- **Criterion A**: Presence of obsessions, compulsions, or both:
  - **Obsessions** defined by (1) Recurrent and persistent thoughts, urges, or images that are experienced as intrusive and unwanted, causing marked anxiety or distress; (2) Individual attempts to ignore or suppress such thoughts/urges/images or neutralize them with some other thought or action.
  - **Compulsions** defined by (1) Repetitive behaviors (e.g., hand washing, ordering, checking) or mental acts (e.g., praying, counting, repeating words silently) performed in response to an obsession or according to rigid rules; (2) Aimed at preventing or reducing anxiety or preventing a dreaded event, but not connected in a realistic way or are clearly excessive.
- **Criterion B**: The obsessions or compulsions are **time-consuming** (e.g., take more than 1 hour per day) or cause clinically significant distress or functional impairment.
- **Criterion C**: Not attributable to substance effects or medical condition.

### Specifiers
- With good or fair insight.
- With poor insight.
- With absent insight / delusional beliefs.
- Tic-related.

### Statistical Metrics
- **12-Month Prevalence**: **1.2%** in the U.S.
- **Lifetime Prevalence**: **2.3%**.
- **Sex Ratio**: Females affected at slightly higher rate in adulthood, but males affected more frequently in childhood.

## 2. Body Dysmorphic Disorder (BDD)
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: F45.22
- **Core Criteria**: Preoccupation with one or more perceived defects or flaws in physical appearance that are not observable or appear slight to others. At some point, the individual has performed repetitive behaviors (e.g., mirror checking, excessive grooming, skin picking) or mental acts (e.g., comparing appearance with others) in response to appearance concerns.

---

## 3. Hoarding Disorder
- **ICD-10-CM Code**: F42.3
- **Core Criteria**: Persistent difficulty discarding or parting with possessions, regardless of their actual value, due to a perceived need to save items and distress associated with discarding them. Results in accumulation of possessions that congest active living areas and compromise their intended use.
