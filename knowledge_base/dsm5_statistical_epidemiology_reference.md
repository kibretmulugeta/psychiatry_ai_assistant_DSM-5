## Key Risk & Prognostic Factors by Diagnostic Category
1. **Genetic / Familial Heritability**: Highest in Autism Spectrum (~85%), Bipolar Disorders (~80%), Schizophrenia (~80%), and ADHD (~75%).
2. **Suicide Risk Coefficients**:
   - Bipolar I Disorder: ~15x general population risk.
   - Major Depressive Disorder: ~20x general population risk in severe cases.
   - Schizophrenia: ~5% - 6% lifetime completed suicide rate.
   - Borderline Personality Disorder: ~8% - 10% completed suicide rate, up to 70% non-suicidal self-injury / attempts.
