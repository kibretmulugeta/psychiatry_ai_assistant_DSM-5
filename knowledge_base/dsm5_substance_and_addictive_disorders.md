# DSM-5 Diagnostic Criteria: Substance-Related and Addictive Disorders

## General Criteria for Substance Use Disorder (SUD)
Applies to specific substances (e.g., Alcohol, Cannabis, Opioids, Sedatives, Stimulants, Tobacco). Defined as a cluster of cognitive, behavioral, and physiological symptoms indicating continued substance use despite significant substance-related problems.

### Symptom Checklist (11 Criteria grouped into 4 Domains)
1. **Impaired Control**:
   - Criterion 1: Substance taken in larger amounts or over a longer period than intended.
   - Criterion 2: Persistent desire or unsuccessful efforts to cut down or control use.
   - Criterion 3: Great deal of time spent in activities necessary to obtain, use, or recover from substance.
   - Criterion 4: **Craving** (strong desire or urge to use substance).
2. **Social Impairment**:
   - Criterion 5: Recurrent use resulting in failure to fulfill major role obligations (work, school, home).
   - Criterion 6: Continued use despite persistent or recurrent social/interpersonal problems.
   - Criterion 7: Important social, occupational, or recreational activities given up or reduced.
3. **Risky Use**:
   - Criterion 8: Recurrent use in situations in which it is physically hazardous (e.g., driving).
   - Criterion 9: Continued use despite knowledge of having a persistent physical/psychological problem caused or exacerbated by substance.
4. **Pharmacological Criteria**:
   - Criterion 10: **Tolerance** (need for markedly increased amounts, or diminished effect with same amount).
   - Criterion 11: **Withdrawal** (characteristic withdrawal syndrome, or substance taken to relieve withdrawal).

### DSM-5 Severity Grading
- **Mild**: Presence of **2 - 3 symptoms**.
- **Moderate**: Presence of **4 - 5 symptoms**.
- **Severe**: Presence of **6 or more symptoms**.

### Specific Substance Codes & Prevalence
- **Alcohol Use Disorder (AUD - F10.10/F10.20)**: 12-month prevalence ~8.5% in U.S. adults.
- **Opioid Use Disorder (OUD - F11.10/F11.20)**: 12-month prevalence ~0.37% in adults.
- **Cannabis Use Disorder (F12.10/F12.20)**: 12-month prevalence ~1.5% in adults, 3.4% in adolescents.
- **Gambling Disorder (F63.0)**: Non-substance behavioral addiction. Requires 4 or more criteria over 12 months.
